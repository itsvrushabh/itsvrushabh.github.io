SYSTEM // KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Omarchy is designed for full keyboard flow. You can navigate, cycle themes, and command the TUI without lifting your hands from the home row.

GENERAL SYSTEM CONTROLS

Ctrl+K
Open Command Palette (Wofi quick launcher)
T
Cycle through 22 official Omarchy themes
M
Toggle ambient background music on/off
S
Toggle mechanical typing sound effects (SFX)
?
Toggle this keyboard shortcuts cheatsheet
` or ~
Jump to & focus interactive TUI terminal
Esc
Close modal / unfocus current input
